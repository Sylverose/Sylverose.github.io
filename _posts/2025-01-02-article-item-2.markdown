# RWD and re-targeting of a private investment dashboard

**Problem description**<br>
Cockpit is a private banking solution, that gives an overview of a client's assets in a web format. Originally developed as a desktop site for bank advisors, Cockpit was scheduled to be re-designed into a new software solution for end-users.<br>The goal was to allow customers of financial institutions to view and follow their assets in real time, on a destop, tablet or smartphone device.<br><br>
I was hired by Vitec Aloc A/S in a role of UX designer, to refine the application in development, primarily with 2 scopes:<br>
✨ to make the application responsive <br>
✨ to reach out to customers, and style the product in their brand (LESS CSS)
<br><br>
**Solution**<br> Responsive web application, with simplified functionality, aiming to give a quick overview on the go.<br><br>
