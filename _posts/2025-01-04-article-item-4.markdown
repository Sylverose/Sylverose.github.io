# Signature cover art and product design

**Rød Måne** <br>
Jette Kjær Petersen's novel was entrusted to me for a full design. This enticing fantasy, aimed at a teenage target group, features a hard cover and handpicked typography and luxurious paper. I illustrated the cover freehand, by using [Clip Studio Paint](https://www.clipstudio.net/en/). The book has been well-received by the audience, and it is one of my favorite works.<br><br> You can purchase your copy [here](https://snepryd.dk/webshop#!/products/r%C3%B8d-m%C3%A5ne). <br><br>

**Brydstykker af et livsstykke**<br>
The author had a picture for the front cover. Unfortunately, it was in low resolution, and tarnished by time. Hence, a big part of the project was photo restoration with Adobe Photoshop. It felt very wholesome to see my client so happy. I also selected the cover style, paper and typography for the book.<br><br> You can purchase the book [here](https://snepryd.dk/webshop#!/products/brudstykker-af-et-livsstykke).<br><br>
<img src="/assets/img/bryd.jpg"><br><br>
**My & Arthur** <br>
This was my first book design project. The beautiful illustrations were previously done by [Maria Nekman Arevad](https://www.instagram.com/maria_arevad?utm_source=ig_web_button_share_sheet&igsh=ZDNlZDc0MzIxNw==). My role was to choose the cover type, materials, paper, typography and edit the book by inserting the illustrations where appropriate with Adobe InDesign. The most challenging part was fitting the received pixel art, primarily on the cover. Originally, the cover illustration was more fit for a thicker book, while the resulted page count was much smaller. I had to edit and rearrange the middle of the illustration, so that the front and back of the cover could fall in place. <br><br>I selected a typography with bigger, rounded vowels, such as "a" and "o"s, which makes it easier for younger children to read.<br> This book makes a wonderful gift for any growing child. I have already sent my copy to my nephew in the UK, and he enjoyed it greatly. Get your copy [here](https://snepryd.dk/webshop#!/products/my-og-arthur).<br><br>
<img src="/assets/img/myogarthur.jpg"><br><br>
**Om 100 dage er det hele bedre**<br>
Pernille's heartfelt journal is one of my most wholesome creations. The author provided me with original illustrations of different styles, which I had to somehow fit together, both on the cover and throughout the book. I wanted to bring out the feeling of a scribble, sketch if you will, but in a tidy and organized typography. Combined with a photo album vibe, it brought some consistency within the chaos of the author's scattered memories and artistic expressions. <br><br>"In 100 days, everything is better" is a journey through sorrow, that brings humans together through the warmth of empathy. <br> I highly recommend it. You can get yours [here](https://snepryd.dk/webshop#!/products/om-100-dage-er-det-hele-bedre). <br><br>
<img src="/assets/img/100dage.jpg"><br><br>
The most significant upsides of this experience was the artistic freedom I was given, in combination with the opportunity to learn all the technical details of book printing, as well as the pleasure of working with the wonderful authors, and the staff at the [Snepryd publishing](https://snepryd.dk/om/team-snepryd).
