# Start-up marketing: Roll-ups and flyers

**The assignment**<br><br>
[VitaSim has](https://www.vitasim.dk/) been making revolutionary changes in the medical and corporate industries through VR training. I was lucky enough to experience their technology first-hand, as an interns while they were still in a start-up phase. <br><br>
I worked closely with their sales team and some of my tasks were to update the website and create marketing materials. <br><br>
<img src="/assets/img/vs/vitasim_flyer.jpg">
<br><br>
The target group consisted primarily of medical students, military and vocational apprentices, otherwise summed-up as an educational sector. In this spirit, I have created materials for public events in the form of flyers and roll-ups, featuring the targeted audience, immersed in VR learning. <br><br>
<img src="/assets/img/vs/vitasim_flyer_front.jpg"> <br><br>
All the printed materials were put together in Adobe InDesign. I assigned the printing of the flyers to a local print house. Since the firm had an office at Cortex Park, a hub for many start-ups often kickstarted by local students, I could use the facility's roll-up printer for free. <br><br>
<img src="/assets/img/vs/print_rollups.jpg"> <br><br>
Printing the roll-up was pretty straight-forward, from a CMYK InDesign document. The challenge was to ensure that the measurements and placement of the graphic elements fitted perfectly with the stand. <br><br>
<img src="/assets/img/vs/vitasim_rollup.jpg"> <br><br>
In order to further spare the start-up some funds, I decided to recycle some old roll-up stands, donated by the University of Southern Denmark.<br> This was a fun and challenging experience, as changing the print on one of these stands is not for the weak of heart. But together we did it, learned from the experience and made the whole promotion package far more affordable than expected.<br><br>
<img src="/assets/img/vs/vitasim_pack.jpg"><br><br>
Everyone was happy with the flyers, and to make their whole networking experience smoother, I also designed new business cards. <br><br>
