# Product design apprenticeship

The project was inspired by the fantastic nature photography book, [Silence of the North](https://mortenhilmer.com/en-dk/products/silence-of-the-north) by Morten Hilmer. <br><br>
The image below is how I describe "nordic silence": <br>
The calm comfort, combined with earthy, muted colors and wool for warmth are the core. From the comfort of my cozy and minimal home, I lock eyes with the winter beyond the lit, wide windows.
<br><br>
<img src="/assets/img/ns/nordic-silence-1.jpg"><br><br>
**Design is born to delight the senses**<br>
If you thought design is not just visual. Design is not art. But art can enhance a design to new heights. <br>
This project was all about artistic expression, and putting it in the context of the targeted audience.<br><br>
**The brainstorming process...**<br>
...started from fun wordplay by association. Inspiration, and the sensation of its formlessness, often come through play, through connecting with ourselves and our environment.<br>This is one thing that differentiates designers from developers: unless we have methods to harness our creativity, sitting at a desk with a timelog in our hands may lead to creative blocks.<br><br> Hence the playful approach with different tools! <br><br>
**The paperplane-ninja and the steel pancake**<br>
Mission no. 1: List 100 random words.<br>
Mission no. 2: Draw 2 pairs of random words and associate them.<br><br>
<img src="/assets/img/ns/nordic-silence-2.jpg"><br><br>
Mission no. 3: Work in pairs. Use scrap to materialize the concept, individually.<br>
